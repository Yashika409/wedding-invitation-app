About this prototype

What works and what is a demonstration

  • Works

    Planner, personalise form, countdown. Everything you type is kept in this browser on this device only. It is not shared and not backed up.

  • Demo only

    RSVP form. It shows a thank-you screen, but nothing is stored or sent. Collecting real replies needs a database or form service.

  • Demo only

    Invitation link. It copies this page's address. Guests who open it see the demo details, not changes you made on your device.

  • Not built

    Accounts, database, email or WhatsApp sending. None of these exist in this prototype.
